# 📅 Programs & Events

Superteam Germany runs programs and event formats that help people move from curiosity to shipping.

For live dates and registrations, always check the Luma calendar first.

- [Superteam Germany on Luma](https://lu.ma/user/superteamde)
- [Website](https://de.superteam.fun/)

## Current Formats

- [Build Station](build-station.md) for Colosseum-focused, high-touch builder support
- [Summit](summit.md) for the flagship annual gathering
- [Dev Guild](dev-guild.md) for developer-heavy livestreams, protocol deep dives, and builder community
- [Creator Guild](creator-guild.md) for creator-focused live sessions and Solana-native culture
- [Coworking Fridays Berlin](https://luma.com/724y4nhb) for recurring in-person building time
- [Coworking Fridays Munich](https://luma.com/yw6nt41q) for recurring in-person building time

Beyond the recurring formats, Superteam Germany also runs bootcamps, brunches, parties, legal office hours, Super Tour stops, and other ecosystem events throughout the year.

For older guild notes, archived sessions, and past hackathon material, use the [Archive](../archive/README.md).
