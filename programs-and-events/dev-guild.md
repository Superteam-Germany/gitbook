# 🧙 Dev Guild

Dev Guild is the builder-first side of Superteam Germany.

It exists to help developers go from “I want to build on Solana” to actually shipping things with the right support, people, and resource stack around them.

The format is a developer-heavy livestream and podcast-style series that runs live on YouTube and X. We regularly invite protocols, builders, and ecosystem teams to go deep on their stack and show how to build with it in practice.

## What You Can Expect

- technical sessions and workshops
- practical discussion around tools, infra, and implementation choices
- support from other builders in the community
- a place to ask questions and find collaborators

## Best For

- developers learning Solana
- hackathon teams that need support
- builders evaluating starter kits and tooling
- people looking for technical peers in Germany
