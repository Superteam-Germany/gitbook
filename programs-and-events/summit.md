# 🏛️ Summit

Summit is Superteam Germany’s flagship annual gathering.

The current edition is [Solana Summit Germany](https://luma.com/solanasummitgermany), happening in June in Berlin.

It is the big community moment where local builders, founders, investors, institutions, creators, and operators meet around the direction of Solana and the ecosystem being built on top of it.

## What You Can Expect

- one large annual gathering instead of a recurring event series
- talks, workshops, founder conversations, and ecosystem updates
- networking across founders, developers, investors, institutions, and creators
- a chance to connect before and after major hackathon cycles
